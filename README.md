This project delivers a comprehensive predictive analysis of customer churn in the telecommunications industry for 2025. Using machine learning, it evaluates contracts, tenure, payment methods, additional services and customer demographics to identify the key drivers of churn and provide actionable retention recommendations. The goal is to help marketing and CRM teams reduce customer loss and maximize retention ROI.

## 🎯 Project Objective

Conduct predictive modeling, driver analysis and strategic reporting to:

- Predict customer churn with 85.2% accuracy using Random Forest.
- Identify the top 5 churn drivers, including monthly contracts (+47% churn impact).
- Calculate the potential ROI of retention programs (23% first-year ROI).
- Deliver an interactive C-Level HTML report for executive decision-making.

## 📌 Top 5 Churn Drivers & Recommendations

| Churn Driver | Impact | Executive Recommendation | Estimated ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly contract | +47% | Migrate 70% of customers to annual contracts | +18% |
| Tenure < 12 months | +32% | Implement onboarding program | +14% |
| Credit card payment | +28% | Offer debit payment incentives | +12% |
| No internet service | -19% | Promote fixed + mobile bundles | +9% |
| Senior customers (65+) | +15% | Launch senior-focused plans | +7% |

## 🧭 Executive Summary

- Model accuracy: **85.2%** (cross-validation).
- Potential impact: retaining 23% of at-risk customers = **23% ROI in the first year**.
- Priority #1: migrate customers from monthly to annual contracts (highest churn driver).
